DNG Declan Woulfe are delighted to offer for sale this exceptional detached home in Duagh, Co. Kerry A Turnkey, B Energy Rated Efficient Home Nestled in the peaceful and picturesque village of Duagh, Co. Kerry, this meticulously maintained, detached home is an ideal choice for a growing family or retired couple seeking a blend of contemporary living and comfort. Offering a high standard of energy efficiency and modern finishes, this property promises to be a true turn-key solution for those wishing to settle into the charm of village life. Interior Features: The interior of this remarkable home has been extensively refurbished to the highest of standards, showcasing an immaculate attention to detail in both design and functionality. The property boasts bright, spacious, and contemporary living accommodation that is sure to impress from the moment you step inside. Upon entry, you are greeted by an expansive, light-filled hallway leading to the various living spaces. The bespoke kitchen is the true heart of the home, featuring modern cabinetry, high-end appliances, and ample counter space. The open-plan layout seamlessly integrates the kitchen with the living and dining areas, making it perfect for both everyday living and entertaining guests. The property offers generous bedrooms that provide a peaceful retreat for all members of the household. Each room has been designed with comfort and tranquility in mind, offering plenty of natural light and ample storage. The luxurious bathrooms are another standout feature, with high-end fixtures, contemporary tiling, and a sense of indulgence in every detail.

Energy Efficiency & Modern Design: This home has been designed with energy efficiency at its core, featuring modern insulation, triple-glazed windows, and an efficient heating system to ensure year-round comfort while keeping energy costs to a minimum.

Exterior & Garden: Externally, the property continues to impress with well-maintained front garden space that is ideal for outdoor relaxation. Whether you enjoy dining al fresco, gardening, or simply enjoying the serene surroundings, the outdoor space offers both privacy and the perfect setting for outdoor living.

Location: Situated in the charming village of Duagh, Co. Kerry, this home enjoys a quiet and tranquil location. The scenic beauty of the region, with its lush green countryside and easy access to the Wild Atlantic Way, offers an ideal balance of village life and rural relaxation. Located on the outskirts of Duagh Village, within walking distance of all amenities. Duagh village benefits from vibrant community, primary school, creche, hardware store, grocery store with filling station, sports clubs etc. The village of Duagh is located equidistant to the towns of Abbeyfeale and Listowel as well as being within commuting distance to Tralee and Limerick City.

Ideal For: Whether you are a family looking for a spacious home in a peaceful yet connected village, or a retired couple seeking comfort and low-maintenance living in a tranquil setting, this property is a rare find. With its combination of luxurious finishes, spacious living, and energy efficiency, it truly offers something for everyone.
